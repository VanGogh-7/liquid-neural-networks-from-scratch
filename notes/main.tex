\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

This book is a companion to the \texttt{liquid-neural-networks-from-scratch} learning repository.
It is designed as a self-contained, first-principles introduction to Liquid Neural Networks, from
continuous-time mathematical foundations through modern scalable architectures.

The book assumes prior knowledge of linear algebra, multivariable calculus, deep learning fundamentals,
and PyTorch. It does \emph{not} assume prior knowledge of ordinary differential equations, dynamical
systems, numerical analysis, or control theory — these are taught as they become necessary.

Each chapter corresponds to one Stage of the curriculum and follows a consistent structure:
historical motivation, mathematical formulation, derivation, implementation, experiments, and discussion.
